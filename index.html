/thesis
Verified-safe autonomy.

      
Vision-Language Model Grounded Safety Verification for Autonomous Vehicle Navigation Using Temporal Logic and Data-Driven Reachability Analysis

      
Bachelor's thesis at TUM Campus Heilbronn, Sep 2026 – Jan 2027. A vision-language model describes the scene; its output is turned into formal temporal-logic rules; reachability analysis checks that a planned motion stays safe before it executes.

      

        
      

      

        
InputCamera framesWhat the vehicle sees

        
PerceptionVision-language modelScene understanding in language

        
GroundingTemporal-logic specsLTL / STL rules a machine can check

        
VerificationHamilton–Jacobi reachabilityCan the motion stay in the safe set?

        
OutputVerified-safe controlOnly safe motion executes
